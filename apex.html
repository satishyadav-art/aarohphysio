Methods

Precision Meets Movement

Manual Therapy

Targeted mobilisation of joints and soft tissues through skilled hands-on treatment. Precise, effective, and felt from the first session.

Functional Training

Sport-specific strength work on the squat rack, cable machine, and kettlebell. Tailored to your goals — not the next patient in line.

Shockwave Therapy

Focused sound waves for chronic tendon pain and tendinopathies. Non-invasive, clinically proven for plantar fasciitis and patellar tendon.

First Contact

Call us, message us, or book directly online. Bring your referral — or come without one as a private patient. Insured or self-pay — everyone is welcome.

02

Assessment & Goals

We listen first. What's causing the pain? What do you want to do again? Together we review your history and define your goal.

Testing

We test mobility, strength, and load tolerance — functionally, not just on paper. Not the calendar decides. Your readiness decides.

60 minutes, just for you — with your therapist. Manual therapy, mobilisation, training, guidance. Every session adapts to where you are today.
