About Us

Dedicated to Your Recovery & Wellness

At Aaroh Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation, we believe every patient deserves personalized, compassionate care. Our team of certified physiotherapists is committed to helping you recover from pain and injury, regain full mobility, and return to the life you love.

We use the latest evidence-based techniques combined with advanced therapeutic equipment to provide the most effective treatment for each individual. From orthopedic injuries to neurological conditions, we're here for you every step of the way.

What We Offer

Our Specialized Services

Comprehensive physiotherapy solutions designed for your complete recovery and lasting health.

Orthopedic Physiotherapy

Treatment of musculoskeletal conditions including fractures, joint pain, ligament injuries, and post-operative recovery.

Most Popular

Neurological Rehabilitation

Specialized therapy for neurological disorders including Parkinson's, multiple sclerosis, and brain injury recovery.

Sports Injury Treatment

Expert care for athletes — from acute sports injuries to chronic overuse conditions — to get you back in the game.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Structured recovery programs to restore strength, mobility, and function following orthopedic and other surgeries.

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Gentle, child-friendly therapy addressing developmental delays, cerebral palsy, and other pediatric conditions.

Electrotherapy & Ultrasound

Advanced modalities including TENS, IFT, and therapeutic ultrasound for effective pain relief and healing.

Spinal & Neck Pain

Targeted treatment for spondylosis, disc herniation, sciatica, and chronic neck and back pain conditions.

Stroke Rehabilitation

Intensive rehabilitation programs helping stroke survivors regain movement, speech, and independence.

Cupping Therapy

Traditional cupping therapy to promote blood flow, ease muscle tension, and accelerate the body's natural healing.

Home Visit Services

Convenient, professional physiotherapy sessions in the comfort of your own home for those unable to travel.
